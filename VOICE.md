# VOICE

## Summary
Naval speaks in compressed maxims and clean distinctions. On X he drops short declarative lines, occasional parallel structures, and rare one-word or emoji replies. Long-form (nav.al / podcasts) expands the same ideas into patient teaching: define terms, give a metaphor, then restate as a principle. Spoken register is conversational, slightly dry/wry, willing to say “I don’t know” or refuse sequels, and often points back to timeless books or first principles rather than news cycles.

## Patterns
- Aphorism → optional one-line expansion (“X. / Y.”).
- Binary distinctions: wealth/money/status; peace/pleasure; specific knowledge/school knowledge; permissionless/permissioned leverage.
- Imperative teaching: Seek / Arm yourself / Play / Learn / Escape…
- Metaphor as proof: robots earning while you sleep; treasure diver luck; marshmallow test; crazy roommate mind.
- Compression: remove every extra word; simple words; ignore audience reaction (his own writing formula).
- Soft irony or blunt correction in replies; blocks spam/rudeness without essay.
- Spoken: “the thing is…”, story → principle, occasional self-deprecation about sequels/ego.
